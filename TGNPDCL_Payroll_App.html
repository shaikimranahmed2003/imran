Getting Started

This application runs entirely inside your browser. All data (employee master, monthly pay runs, LOP, leave, increments, other entries) is saved to this computer's local disc (browser Local Storage). Nothing is sent to any server.
  • Master – maintain the employee master with all earning/deduction heads.
  • Month Processing – open a payroll month, compute pay for every employee, and Finalize (close) the month; then use "Start Next Month" to roll forward.
  • LOP & Leave – record Loss-of-Pay days and leave sanctions per employee per month.
  • Other Earning/Deduction – one-off or date-ranged earnings/deductions (arrears, recoveries, etc.) with a start & end date; they auto-apply to any month they overlap.
  • Annual Grade Increment – record increments with an effective date; Basic Pay used in processing automatically reflects the latest increment effective on/before the processing month.
  • Abstract & Payslip – GL-code-wise abstract report and individual payslips, both printable.
⚠ Local Storage lives inside this browser only. Use the Backup (JSON) button regularly to save a copy of your data to a file, and Restore to load it back (e.g. on another computer or after clearing browser data).

Leave & LOP Sanction

Record any sanctioned leave, including Loss of Pay, from one unified form. Choose Loss of Pay (LOP) as the Type to have it reduce the employee's pay for whichever month(s) the range falls in — a range spanning more than one month is split automatically. Proration rule: Sanctioned Days = Days in Month − LOP Days. Every earning head — including Basic Pay, DA, HRA and CCA — is paid in the ratio Sanctioned Days ÷ Days in Month, applied individually to each earning line (not as one lumpsum deduction at the end), except D.A. Arrears, which is always paid in full regardless of LOP. Any other leave type is recorded for reference only and does not affect pay.
